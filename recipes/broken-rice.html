Broken Rice

Description

This broken rice plate is made of homemade ingredients.

Cơm Tấm (Vietnamese broken rice) features broken jasmine rice, sweet-savory grilled pork chops, steamed egg meatloaf (chả trứng), shredded pork skin (bì), scallion oil (mỡ hành), pickled daikon and carrots, fresh cucumbers, tomatoes, and a sweet fish sauce dressing (nước mắm).

Ingredients

  • Rice: Broken jasmine rice and a little oil.
  • Grilled Pork: Pork chops, lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, honey, and five-spice.
  • Sides: Ground pork, wood ear mushrooms, vermicelli noodles, and eggs (for the meatloaf); boiled pork skin tossed in toasted rice powder (thính).
  • Garnish & Sauce: Scallions, oil, pickled carrots/daikon, and diluted fish sauce (nước chấm).

Steps

  1. Cook the Rice: Rinse broken rice, mix with water (roughly a 1:1 ratio) and a splash of oil, then steam or cook in a rice cooker until fluffy.
  2. Marinate and Grill Pork: Mix minced lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, honey, and spices. Coat the pork chops, marinate for hours, and grill until caramelized.
  3. Make the Chả (Meatloaf): Combine ground pork, soaked and chopped wood ear mushrooms, glass noodles, eggs, and seasonings. Steam until set, brush an egg yolk on top, and steam briefly again.
  4. Prepare Bì and Mỡ Hành: Boil pork skin, slice into thin strips, and toss with roasted rice powder (thính). Heat chopped green onions with oil in a pan or microwave for the scallion oil.
  5. Assemble:Mound the broken rice on a plate, top with the grilled pork chop, a slice of egg meatloaf, a scoop of shredded pork skin, scallion oil, pickled veggies, and serve with sweet fish sauce on the side.
