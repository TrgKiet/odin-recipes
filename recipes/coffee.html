Coffee

Description

I love having a cup of Coffee in the morning.

To make a standard hot black or milk coffee at home, you need 1 to 2 teaspoons of instant coffee (or 2 tablespoons of ground coffee), 1 cup (200 ml) of hot water, and optional sugar or milk to taste.

Ingredients

  • Coffee: 1–2 tsp instant coffee or 2 tbsp ground coffee beans
  • Water: 1 cup (200 ml) hot water (just off the boil)
  • Add-ins (Optional): Sugar, honey, milk, or cream

Steps

  1. Add coffee: Put the coffee grounds or instant coffee into your mug.
  2. Pour water: Add the hot water over the coffee.
  3. Stir: Mix well for 10 seconds until the coffee and any added sugar dissolve.
  4. Pour in your choice of milk, cream, or sweetener if you do not like it black.
