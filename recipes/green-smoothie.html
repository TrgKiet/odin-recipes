Green Smoothie

Description

This green smoothie is made of natural ingredients.

To make a basic green smoothie, blend 1 cup of almond milk, 2 cups of fresh spinach, 1 frozen banana, and 1 cup of frozen mango or pineapple on high for 60 seconds until smooth and creamy.

Ingredients

  • Liquid (1 cup): Almond milk, oat milk, coconut water, or regular milk.
  • Greens (2 cups): Fresh or frozen spinach (mildest flavor) or kale.
  • Fruit (1 to 1.5 cups, preferably frozen): Banana, mango, pineapple, or peaches for natural sweetness and thickness.
  • Optional Boosters (1 tbsp): Chia seeds, flaxseed, nut butter, or protein powder.

Steps

  1. Pour Liquid: Put the liquid in the blender first so the blades spin easily.
  2. Add Greens: Put the spinach or kale on top of the liquid.
  3. Add Fruit & Extras: Add your frozen fruit and optional seeds or protein powder.
  4. Blend: Start on low speed, then turn to high.
  5. Blend for 50 to 60 seconds until completely smooth, then pour and drink.
